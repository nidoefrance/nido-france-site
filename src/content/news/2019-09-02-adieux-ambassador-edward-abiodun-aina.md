Nigerians and friends of the Federal Republic Nigeria in France announce with regret the call to glory of His Excellency Ambassador Edward Abiodun AINA, OON, on Thursday 29th August 2019. He was a retired first class professional Ambassador and career Diplomat, an attentive listening ear, a pragmatic and rigorous person, he was also a very supportive, fine and jovial gentleman. Nigerians in Diaspora Organisation Europe-France (NIDOE-France) is, because Ambassador AINA was!!! He was a vital facilitator during the creation and official launch of NIDOE-France on May 26, 2001 on a platform provided by the Union of Nigerian Engineers & Scientists from France (UNESF).

We share these difficult moments with his family, all the local staff of the embassy of Nigeria Paris who served at his time, his colleagues and friends in the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and all well wishers. May his soul rest in perfect peace.

(Notre cher Patron et ami, les grands hommes ne meurent jamais, merci pour tout, on vous aime très fort, reposez en paix)
