Dr Engr. Martin OKEKE, the first executive President of Nigerians in Diaspora Organisation Europe, France chapter (NIDOE-France), pioneer member of the NIDO Europe Protem committee, just passed on this 19th June 2019, after a brief illness in Paris region, France. Dr OKEKE is a quiet gentleman whose contribution to the NIDO family is inestimable. The most successful Nigerian fashion show in 2004 and the unprecedented Nigerian cultural week 2006 took place under his chairmanship. Our thoughts go to his immediate family. We share the pains with them and send our heart felt condolence. May his soul rest in perfect peace. Thank you Martin for everything. Rest in peace.
