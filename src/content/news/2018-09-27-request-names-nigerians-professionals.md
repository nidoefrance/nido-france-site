The Embassy of Nigeria in France informed that the National Bureau of Statistics in Nigeria is requesting for the data of Nigerians in Diaspora indicating their occupation, sex and duration of stay between the periods 2015-2018. In this regard please fill up the contact form on the home page and leave in details:

- Full Names
- Email
- Phone number
- In the field "Message" (Occupation, position held between 2015 and 2018)

Thank you all for your cooperation.
